Hi, I'm nacfson, a software engineer based in Korea.

AI is reshaping tech companies, and the way we work, faster than anyone expected. So I've decided to start writing down my thoughts and visions and leave them as a digital footprint.

### Why a blog?

Everywhere I look, people say "developers will disappear" or "AI will change everything." I don't believe the developer role will vanish, but I'm certain it won't look like it used to. AI will change the world in ways we haven't imagined, and I'd rather ride that wave than watch it pass.

### This blog is where I'll:

organize my thinking about engineering in the AI era,
record my decisions, goals, and long-term vision, so my future self (and you) can look back on them,
share what I learn, in case it helps someone else.


I believe that sharing knowledge openly is one small way to make people's lives better. 

**That belief, a kind of humanism, is the real reason I'm writing.**
